Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how Sweet Crumbs collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information you provide when placing an order, creating an account, or contacting customer support. This may include your name, email address, shipping address, phone number, and payment details.

How We Use Your Data

We use your information to process orders, deliver products, answer questions, and send updates about promotions, delivery status, and account activity.

Security & Sharing

We protect your data with industry-standard security practices. We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with trusted partners only when necessary to fulfill your order.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve user experience, remember preferences, and analyze site traffic. You can manage cookie settings through your browser.
