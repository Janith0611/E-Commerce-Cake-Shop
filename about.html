Baking with Passion

Founded in 2011, Sweet Crumbs started as a small family kitchen with a big dream: to create cakes that not only look spectacular but taste unforgettable.

We believe that every celebration deserves a centerpiece made with the finest ingredients, meticulous attention to detail, and a whole lot of love. From our rich Belgian chocolate to our fresh locally sourced berries, we never compromise on quality.
