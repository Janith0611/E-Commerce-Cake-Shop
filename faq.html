Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about ordering, shipping, and caring for your Sweet Crumbs cake.

Ordering

How far in advance should I place my cake order?

For standard cakes, please order at least 72 hours before your event. Custom designs and specialty cakes may require 5-7 days notice.

Can I request a custom design or flavor?

Yes! Select "Custom Cake" on the shop page, then choose your flavor and design options. Our team will confirm the details with you.

Shipping & Delivery

Do you deliver cakes locally?

We offer local delivery within our city limits and curbside pickup for neighbors. Delivery fees depend on your location at checkout.

What happens if my cake arrives damaged?

If your cake arrives damaged, please contact us immediately with photos. We will evaluate the issue and help arrange a refund or replacement.

Product Care

How should I store my cake?

Keep your cake refrigerated if it contains fresh cream, buttercream, or fruit filling. Remove it from the fridge 30-45 minutes before serving for best taste.

How long will my cake stay fresh?

Most cakes stay fresh for 2-3 days refrigerated. Cakes with perishable fillings should be eaten within 24-48 hours.
